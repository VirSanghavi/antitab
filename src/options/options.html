Antitab

Sites you allow are told the tab is still in front, so their video and audio keep going while you are somewhere else.

One step to go

Antitab starts with access to nothing. Open a site that pauses on you, click the Antitab icon in the toolbar, and switch it on there. It takes effect straight away, with no reload.

How it pretends

Applies to every site you switch on.

Antitab runs entirely on your machine. No accounts, no network requests, no data leaves the browser. The site list lives in local extension storage and nowhere else.
